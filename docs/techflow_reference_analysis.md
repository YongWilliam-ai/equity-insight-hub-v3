# BIT Daily Hub Upgrade — Reference Analysis

## Reference reviewed

The reference is TechFlow's 25 August 2026 Bitget UEX daily report: <https://techflowpost.com/article/33488>. It is an editorial and information-architecture reference only. Its secondary reporting, analyst commentary, market data, crypto metrics and non-primary claims must not be converted into BIT facts without independent verification.

## What works in the reference

| Pattern | Reader value | BIT adoption decision |
|---|---|---|
| Three-part opening news stack | Separates policy, macro and cross-asset headlines before price recap. | Adopt as a compact **Signal Stack** with source tier and confidence labels. |
| Cross-asset recap | Explains whether rates, commodities, FX and crypto confirm or contradict equity price action. | Adopt as **Cross-asset Conditions**; use only verified data and show `not available at cutoff` rather than invent a quote. |
| Index, megacap and sector layers | Gives readers a fast market map before stock detail. | Adopt as a **Market Scoreboard**, **Megacap Tape** and **Sector / Industry Read-through** sequence. |
| Five-stock deep dives | Creates repeatable, skimmable company analysis. | Adopt the five-question BIT card: what happened, why it matters, price reaction, read-through, next proof. |
| Calendar and watch conditions | Converts news collection into an observable next-session checklist. | Retain and expand the existing **This Week** section with time zone, evidence tier and positive/negative conditions. |

## What BIT should not copy

BIT's product should not become a crypto-market call sheet, a list of trade ideas, or a stream of unqualified headlines. It must retain three distinctions that the reference does not fully solve:

1. **Research evidence versus market commentary.** Every factual card needs source tier, timestamp and an explicit uncertainty label when evidence is incomplete.
2. **United States / Hong Kong clock separation.** U.S. completed close, HK completed close, HK intraday snapshot and next U.S. catalysts must never be blended into one apparent market timestamp.
3. **Internal research rather than trade instruction.** The product explains drivers and validation conditions; it does not state guaranteed outcomes, personalised suitability or execution instructions.

## Recommended BIT information architecture

1. **Research Summary:** one market thesis, data cutoff and evidence state.
2. **Signal Stack:** policy, macro, cross-asset and earnings signals ranked by relevance.
3. **Market Scoreboard:** U.S. indices, rates, breadth and verified cross-asset confirmations.
4. **U.S. Research:** index split, sector / industry read-through, five-question company cards and heatmap.
5. **Hong Kong Dual Clock:** prior completed close, live snapshot (if available), 09:00–16:00 HKT market-window status, China/HK catalysts and explicit handoff to the next U.S. session.
6. **Cross-market Transmission:** conditional links, not claimed causality.
7. **This Week:** official event calendar with positive / negative / unresolved checks.
8. **Evidence Ledger:** tiers, access status, limitations and internal-use disclosure.

## Fixed URL / edition model

The canonical report URL remains the repository root: `https://yongwilliam-ai.github.io/equity-insight-hub-v3/`. It always loads the latest approved report. Date query URLs remain durable editions, for example `?date=2026-08-24&lang=TW`. A visible **Edition Archive** control should list prior published dates and link to those date URLs; it is an archive navigator, not an alternative live home page.
