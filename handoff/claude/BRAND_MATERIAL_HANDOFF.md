# BIT Brand Material Handoff

| Asset / rule | Repository location or implementation | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Official black wordmark | `client/public/brand/BIT-Logo-black.svg` | White-page header. |
| Official blue BIT app mark | `client/public/brand/BIT-Logo-blue.svg` | Approved compact brand mark where used. |

| BIT White / neutral surfaces | `#FFFFFF`, `#F5F7FA`, neutral borders | Research reading surface and low-distraction exhibits. |
| UED control / motion rules | `client/src/index.css` | Compact controls, 8px / 12px components, short restrained transitions. |

## Market-performance exception

Market direction must not borrow the BIT Blue brand color. Heatmaps use:

| Return semantics | Palette |
|---|---|
| Positive | Light teal through dark green. |
| Near zero | Neutral grey. |
| Negative | Light coral through orange-red / deep red. |

This exception is mandatory because a user must immediately read return direction without confusing UI branding for a market gain.

## Intentionally not used in daily research cards

Weekly Market Watch light artwork, 3D / glass marketing treatments, and campaign-oriented imagery are available materials but are not applied to the daily market research cards. The research product prioritizes factual readability, source traceability, and compact comparison over campaign visual impact.
